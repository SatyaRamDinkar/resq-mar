Key Policy Note
As per the university IPR policy, there is no limit on the number of patents that can be filed.
Prototype Development (Mandatory): A functional prototype, proof-of-concept, simulation model, or experimental validation demonstrating the feasibility of the invention must be developed. Purely conceptual submissions without technical validation will not be considered.

Mandatory Details (For Utility Patents – While Filling Google Form)
Please ensure you provide the following:

Plan to commercialize the invention, pursue product development, or start a business venture.
Mention the industry domain aligned with the invention (e.g., Healthcare, AgriTech, etc.).
Identify potential industries interested in collaboration and development of the invention.
  Review and Approval Process
The IPR Team will check the completeness of your submission.
A committee meeting will be scheduled for evaluation.
You are advised to give a presentation before the committee.
Based on the committee’s decision, your application may be:
Recommended
Revision
Not Recommended
If approval, the application will be processed further for patent filing.
All faculty members are encouraged to actively participate in patent filing and innovation activities. The IPR Cell will support you throughout the process.
