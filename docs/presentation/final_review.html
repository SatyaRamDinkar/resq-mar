4-Step Agentic RAG: Retrieve -> Assess -> Re-retrieve -> Final

Step 1: Retrieve top-5 SOPs from ChromaDB

Step 2: AssessorAgent scores relevance (0-1)

Step 3: If score < 0.7, re-retrieve with refined query

Step 4: Return highest-confidence SOP to Planner

Results

Naive RAG Coverage: 0.47

Agentic RAG Coverage: 0.82

Improvement: +74%

Assessment step filters noise; re-retrieval fixes low-confidence gaps.
What to SAY: Naive RAG often pulls the wrong manuals if keywords overlap. We introduced a 4-step Agentic RAG loop. An AssessorAgent acts as a quality filter, rejecting bad pulls and forcing a re-query. This bumped our standard operating procedure coverage from 47% to 82% -- a massive 74% improvement in safety. TIME: 50 seconds TRANSITION: Once we have the plan, we need to route vehicles.

Event-Triggered Routing: Solve Only When Needed

 [Idle] --new incident--> [Solve]
 [Solve] --no change--> [Idle]
 [Solve] --threshold exceeded--> [Re-solve]

Results

Continuous Re-routing: 15 calls

AET Adaptive: 5 calls

Reduction: 66.7%

Road blocked? Re-solve. Otherwise, reuse. Saves compute AND time.
What to SAY: Standard systems re-run complex math solvers every single time a new call comes in. This crashes servers during mass disasters. Our Adaptive Event-Triggered (AET) routing holds routes steady unless a major threshold is crossed. This reduced solver calls by 66.7%, preserving vital CPU cycles. Where trucks cannot go, drones fly. Where drones cannot carry, trucks haul.
What to SAY: If an earthquake blocks a road, ambulances cannot reach the victims. Drones can, but they have short battery lives. We built a collaborative solver where trucks act as mobile launchpads. The truck drives to the blockage edge, and the drone handles the final mile. This guarantees 100% geographic coverage. Future Work
